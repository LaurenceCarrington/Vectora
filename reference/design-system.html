Preview before removing

Dimensions & callouts

Material & process preview

The top-toolbar cube opens a lazy-loaded 3D view of finished pieces. Closed Cut contours define parts, nested holes and islands; open cuts form narrow grooves. Engrave and Raster Engrave recess filled regions or path strokes to separate depths, clipped to the remaining material. Overlaps use the deepest operation. Engraving alone gets a fitted blank. Artwork colours can be overlaid without changing the document. Camera controls, labels, borders and hover states use the same neutral and accent tokens as the rest of the UI; the model and scene grid retain their own preview colours.

The viewport background, dimensional grid, camera controls and empty/error states follow Light, Dark or High contrast mode, including theme changes while open. Material appearance is independent of the interface theme. Starts in Top view. Navigation follows Online3DViewer: left-drag rotates with fixed upright axes, middle/right-drag or Shift-drag pans, and wheel or Ctrl/Cmd-drag zooms. One-finger touch rotates; two fingers pan and pinch. Rotation sensitivity stays consistent across viewport sizes. Fit recentres the model while preserving its viewing angle; Reset returns to Top. Camera icons use consistent cubes with highlighted Top, Back, Front, Left and Right faces, an unfilled Isometric cube, a framed cube for Fit and a circular arrow for Reset. View changes use short eased transitions (instant with reduced motion). Arrow keys rotate, +/− zoom, F fits and Home resets. Rendering runs only while the view changes. Camera clipping planes track the nearest and farthest model bounds to preserve depth precision on large, thin sheets; the grid stays behind the material at close zoom. Preview input is preflighted at 10,000 visible objects, 50,000 contours and 120,000 source segments.
